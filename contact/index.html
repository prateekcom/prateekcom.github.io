What happens next No sales sequence
  1. Within 2 days

    A written reply

    From a person who has read it. Usually with a question or two, because the first description is rarely the real problem.

  2. A short call

    Thirty to forty minutes

    We ask what you are actually trying to change. You get an honest read on whether this is worth doing and whether we are the right people.

  3. If it fits

    A written scope

    What the work is, what it delivers, roughly how long, and what we need from you. Priced before it starts.

  4. If it does not

    We say so

    Sometimes the answer is a query, a report, or a different firm. You get that answer rather than a proposal.
