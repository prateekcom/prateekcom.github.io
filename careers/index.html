What the work is actually like Worth knowing before you apply

You will see real production systems, not sandboxes.

Client environments, real data, real constraints. The mess is the job — a tidy problem would not have needed us.

You will write things down.

Evaluations, architecture decisions, handover documentation. Work that cannot be explained cannot be handed over, and everything we do gets handed over.

You will sometimes recommend against the project.

Saying "this does not need AI" is part of the role, not a failure of it. We would rather lose the work than build the wrong thing.

You will talk to the people who do the work.

Engineers here sit in discovery sessions. The person who watched an agent work for an afternoon usually spots the real problem before the spec does.

How hiring runs Four steps, no surprises
Step 01

Apply

Send us something real — a project, a repository, a piece of writing. A CV is fine but it is rarely the interesting part.

Step 02

Conversation with the team

How you think, what you have built, and whether the way we work suits the way you work. Both directions.

Step 03

Technical round

A real problem rather than a puzzle. We are more interested in how you reason about an ambiguous brief than in whether you memorised an algorithm.

Step 04

Offer

Terms in writing, questions answered honestly, and a start date that works for you.

You ask, we answer The usual questions
What do you look for in candidates?

Skill, curiosity and the ability to work with an unclear brief. Most of what we do starts as a vague sentence from someone who is not technical, so the ability to ask the second and third question matters as much as what you can build.

Do you take fresh graduates?

Yes. We have hired people straight out of university and people fifteen years in. What matters is evidence that you have built something and can explain the decisions you made.

Is there an internship?

We have run summer internships before and expect to again. Apply through the same form and say what you are looking for — mention your availability window.

Remote or in office?

Both work. The team is based in Nagpur and we are remote-friendly. Discovery and handover phases benefit from being in a room together, so occasional travel comes up.

How do I ask about campus placements?

Write to hello@vibencode.com with your institution and the timeline you are working to.
