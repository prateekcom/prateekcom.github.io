    /ˈvʌɪb.ɪn.kəʊd/ verb — from vibe, an intent felt before it is said, and encode, to make a thing executable
    1. 1 To take the thing someone can only describe — the hunch, the pressure, the half-sentence in a board meeting — and make it something a system can actually run. “They vibencoded a year of support tickets into an assistant that cites its sources.”
    2. 2 The work that happens between “what if” and “it’s live” — the part no demo shows you. Strategy, applied research, and hands-on engineering for GenAI, agent orchestration and sovereign AI.
