About

A team to work with.

We spent years building software for other people and got good at the unglamorous half: the integration, the edge cases, the handover. That is the half AI projects keep failing at, so it is the half we sell. The rest of our time goes to our own products and the people coming up behind us.

Building since 2021 Indore, Madhya Pradesh
What we are And what we are not

A deep-tech consulting and engineering practice. We advise, investigate, design and build, and we stay until the thing runs on a Monday without us.

Not a platform, not a licence, not a seat. There is nothing to subscribe to. You hire people, for a period, to do a defined piece of work, and you keep what they build. Most of them are startups, which is why nothing we leave behind assumes a platform team on the other side of it.

How we work Six things we actually hold to
  • Say the uncomfortable thing earlyIf the use case is wrong, or the data is not there, you should hear it in week one rather than week nine.
  • Measure before buildingThe evaluation gets written before the interface. A capability nobody can assess is a liability.
  • Recommend against ourselvesSome problems do not need AI. Saying so costs us one project and earns the next three.
  • Design for the handoverWe work so that leaving is uneventful. Documentation, tests and a team that has been doing it alongside us.
  • Keep the human where it mattersApproval sits where money, data or reputation changes hands. Autonomy is earned, not assumed.
  • Ship, then widenBehind a flag, on real traffic, one team first. Big-bang launches are how you find out what you got wrong all at once.
Lean on purpose Why the team is the size it is

A small team is not a stage we are trying to grow out of.

Most of what goes wrong on one of these projects goes wrong in the gap between the person who understood the problem and the person who writes the code. Every layer you add widens it. The widest version is an agency with a sales team at one end and a delivery pod at the other, joined by a document.

So the person who scopes your engagement is on it. The founders are on it. Nobody is briefed through a specification, because there are few enough people involved that a specification is not the only way the understanding can travel.

It also means we can only sell what somebody here actually wants to work on. There is no bench to keep busy. In a field that moves at this speed that is not a constraint, it is the mechanism: the depth is a by-product of people following what they find genuinely interesting, and it does not survive being assigned.

The cost is that we cannot take everything that comes in. That is the trade, and we would make it again.
