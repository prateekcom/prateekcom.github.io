Privacy policy

What we hold, and why.

We take data privacy seriously and would rather be plain about it: what is stored, how it is used, and how it is kept secure.

01About

Vibencode Private Limited operates vibencode.com (hereinafter referred to as “Vibencode”, “The Company”). The following Privacy Policy outlines the privacy considerations enacted by Vibencode relating to the use of your personal information.

Vibencode Private Limited is a privately held company incorporated under the Companies Act 2013 of the Government of the Republic of India. The Company is endorsed as a startup entity by the Startup India division of the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade.

02Data collection, onward transfer and processing

Vibencode may collect, store, use and disclose information about individuals which may constitute personal data (including sensitive personal data under various Government Laws e.g. Indian IT Act Privacy rules, GDPR, etc.) for lawful, explicit, and legitimate purposes and for further processing of personal data consistent with those purposes.

Vibencode shall obtain consent from the data subject wherever appropriate and required for the processing of personnel information prior to collecting, storing, and processing any personal data.

Vibencode shall not utilize an individual’s personal data beyond the scope granted without prior consent from the individual and shall take measures to ensure that this principle is observed. An individual’s personal data shall not be provided or otherwise disclosed to third parties other than our affiliates, investigators, or law enforcement personnel when consent has been obtained from the individual in question or when disclosure is legally mandated. To the extent permitted by applicable law, The Company may record and monitor electronic and voice communications to ensure compliance with the legal and regulatory obligations and internal policies and for the purposes outlined above.

Any transfer of personal data to a third party shall take place only if all necessary controls for data protection are applied by the third party in order to ensure that the level of protection of personal data is guaranteed.

03Confidentiality and security

The Company takes prudent steps to safeguard the confidentiality and security of all personal data including taking procedural and organizational steps to protect personal data. These steps include but are not limited to entering into written agreements with all its vendors who process personal data which confirms with necessary data protection regulations.

In addition, Vibencode strives to protect personally identifiable information that it maintains or disseminates so it is not obtained by unauthorized individuals or used in unauthorized ways, including through the use of appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. E.g. policies and procedures, physical and logical access controls, and encryptions.

04Data subject access, correction and deletion

Vibencode recognizes the right of data subjects at reasonable intervals to seek or request a copy of the personal data held in relation to them by the Company. If any personal data is found to be wrong, the individual concerned has the right to ask us to amend, update, or delete it, as appropriate. In some circumstances, individuals also have a right to object to processing their personal data as per the prevailing laws. The Company shall process the personal data solely for the business purposes for which it was intended.

Privacy consent can be withdrawn easily and at any time by the data subject by informing the appropriate authority within the company or emailing us at legal.vibencode@gmail.com. Personal data will not be retained or stored for a period more than necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy statement. The personal data shall be deleted from the systems after evaluating if there are any legal, statutory, contractual, or legitimate purpose that expects the company to retain data.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at legal.vibencode@gmail.com.
